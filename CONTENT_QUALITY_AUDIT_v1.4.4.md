## Provenance and rights safeguards
- External archive pages are presented as third-party sources, not as official Ministry endpoints.
- A source page's claim that a correction is official is not treated as proof by the app.
- No third-party PDFs, scans, or answer keys are redistributed in this release.
- Each document must be checked for source, exam year/session, subject, answer-key provenance, and reuse permission before local ingestion.
- Historical papers require a separate review before claiming alignment with the 2026–2027 curriculum.
