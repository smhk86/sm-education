# SM SCHOOL AI v1.6.7 — Release report

## Delivered
- Local adaptive analysis groups recorded results by subject for the active learner.
- Fewer than three scored activities are labelled insufficient data; mastery labels are advisory only.
- Weekly plan prioritizes up to three lowest-performing subjects and rotates them through seven study sessions.
- Session completion is stored per learner.
- Subject practice shortcut opens the existing exercise bank at the learner grade.
- Adaptive report can be exported as CSV.
- Adaptive plans are included in local JSON backup/restore and removed with the learner profile.

## Privacy and limitations
- Adaptive analysis and plans are stored in localStorage on this device. They are not sent to Groq or a server.
- Accuracy is calculated from existing recorded activities; it cannot identify a specific skill or lesson unless that detail was recorded by the existing activity.
- The seven-day plan is a suggested schedule, not an official Ministry curriculum timetable.
- This release does not claim APK build or device UI testing.
