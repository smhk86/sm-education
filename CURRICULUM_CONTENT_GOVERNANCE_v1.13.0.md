# Curriculum content governance — v1.13.0

Every learning resource must carry:
- academic_year (e.g. 2026-2027)
- stage, grade, stream/pathway where applicable
- subject, term, unit, lesson
- competency / learning objective
- resource_type (lesson, exercise, assessment, integrative situation)
- source_title, source_url or document identifier, page_number
- verification_status: draft | under_review | verified | rejected
- reviewer, reviewed_at, rights_status

Rules:
1. Never label content official without a traceable official source and page/section.
2. Keep platform-created practice distinct from ministry exam papers.
3. Repeated questions must be reviewed for grade-appropriate wording and cognitive demand.
4. Secondary content must be pathway-aware for 1AS common trunks and 2AS/3AS streams.
5. Keep a coverage report by grade × subject × term × competency; missing cells remain explicitly missing.
6. Amazigh, arts, music and physical education are included only with level-appropriate, source-reviewed content.
