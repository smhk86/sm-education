## Base and preservation
- Continued from the supplied `SM_SCHOOL_AI_v1.7.5.zip`.

- Updated package version and service-worker cache key to v1.7.6.

## Added
- Parent weekly review now includes a suggested seven-day home study plan.
- The plan prioritizes subjects whose recorded accuracy is below 60% after at least five answers, consistent with the existing parent-review threshold.
- When no subject needs support, it rotates through subjects with recorded activity; with no activity it prompts the parent to choose a subject.
- The seven stages rotate through a short lesson, graded exercises, mistake correction, recall, a short quiz, an applied/integrative task, and light review.
- Plan labels and tasks are localized in Arabic, French, and English.
- The plan is explicitly a home-learning suggestion, not an official Ministry timetable or assessment.
