# Content quality audit — v1.5.0

- Existing platform bank: 1,167 exercises (1,155 MCQ + 12 numeric), 315 term exams and 315 integrative situations; unchanged.
- Added searchable local catalogue API for excerpts manually approved after their source document is recorded as rights-cleared.
- Search filters: free text, grade, subject, term, exam year and exam type.
- Candidate metadata includes exam year and type; these are reviewer-entered, not inferred as official facts.
- Catalogue does not serve original PDF files and does not insert records into the exercise bank.
- Official alignment remains `not_verified`; suggested answer verification remains `human_review_recorded_not_independently_certified`.
- No official PDFs were downloaded in this build; the catalogue is empty until source files are imported and reviewed.
- No accounts, cloud sync or APK build.
