# Content quality audit — v1.5.7

## Scope
- Local CSV intake and evidence validation for 2026-2027 annual curriculum progressions.
- No curriculum lesson, competency, subject matrix, coefficient, or exercise was promoted to official status.
- Evidence rows remain in the user's current browser local storage and can be exported as CSV.

## Verification rules
Reviewer-marked verified rows require grade/stage/subject/term, an HTTPS URL on `education.gov.dz` or a subdomain, official document title, positive page number, unit, lesson, competency, source publication date, reviewer role/name, and verification date.

## Known limitations
- Browser-local review is not a secure institutional approval workflow and does not synchronize across devices.
- The source URL host check does not prove that a document is authentic, current, or applicable; a human must inspect the referenced page and publication.
- The official annual progression matrix remains unverified until documents are reviewed and a later release explicitly integrates approved evidence.
- Existing platform exercise content remains supplemental, not official Ministry content.
