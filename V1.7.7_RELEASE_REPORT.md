# SM SCHOOL AI v1.7.7 — Release report

## New in this release
- Added a local, learner-specific learning diagnostic covering the latest 30 days.
- Aggregates activity by subject and classifies evidence as priority support, developing, secure, or insufficient.
- Requires at least five recorded answers before classifying a subject as needing support.
- Adds a seven-day remediation sequence: lesson, practice, recall, short assessment, error review, practical application, and recheck.
- Integrated the diagnostic panel into the Home School page for each learner profile.
- Arabic, French and English interface copy; no remote transfer of learner progress.

## Safeguards
- Uses only locally recorded progress and filters by learner ID.
- Excludes future-dated and older-than-30-day records.
- Treats the result as advisory, not an official assessment or diagnosis.
- Does not label a subject as weak when evidence is insufficient.
